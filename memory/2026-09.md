## 2026-09-10: Hard freeze postmortem — fan control + watchdog + kometa fix

Root cause unresolved by nature of hard hangs; trigger = deep-context decode path; candidate mechanisms: TTM/GTT eviction livelock (cgroup mem_limit blind to driver-side GTT pressure — gates worked Aug 30, silent here) vs GPU ring wedge. Session grew 84K→100K over the afternoon (same conversation, LCP 1.0).
**Corsair precedent (same Sixunited AXB35 board):** strix-halo-guide issue #24 — silent hard locks under sustained inference on 3 Corsair AI WS 300 nodes; confounder = kernel update without out-of-tree fan module → stock EC curve → 180 W/111 °C events; controlled 93 °C soaks didn't reproduce; independent V32 board couldn't reproduce either. Emergency dial if hangs recur: **SCLK 2400 MHz cap** (−0.9% decode / −6.4% prefill / −20 W / −9 °C, harness in THERMAL_STABILITY.md).
**Stock fan curve was the thermal villain:** 60% duty at ALL temps (2785–3071 rpm idle), 100% only at 97 °C. Idle temp is actually ~48 °C (earlier 75–80 °C readings were model-load transients 2 min post-boot — correction).
**Deployed:**
